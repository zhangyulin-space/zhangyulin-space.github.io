**Paper**
- Title: Do Large Language Models Know What Humans Know?
- Authors: Sean Trott, Cameron Jones, Tyler Chang, James Michaelov, Benjamin K. Bergen
- Year: 2023
- Link: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/cogs.13309

**Overview**
- Examines alignment between LLM knowledge and human judgments across cognitive tasks.
- Positions LLMs as instruments for cognitive discovery.

**Key Ideas**
- Mapping NLP tasks to psycholinguistic phenomena (metaphor, ambiguity).
- Using LLM outputs to probe cognitive hypotheses and biases.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Comparative analyses of model outputs vs. human data across selected tasks.

**Limitations**
- Training data confounds may drive apparent alignment.
- External validity varies by task type.

**Discussion Questions**
- When do LLMs mirror human judgments, and why?
- How to control for corpus artifacts in cognitive probes?
- What experimental designs best leverage LLMs for theory testing?
- Ethical considerations in interpreting model cognition.
