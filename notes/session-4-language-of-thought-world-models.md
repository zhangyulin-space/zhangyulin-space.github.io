**Paper**
- Title: From Word Models to World Models: Translating from Natural Language to the Probabilistic Language of Thought
- Authors: Lionel Wong, Gabriel Grand, Alexander K. Lew, Noah D. Goodman, Vikash K. Mansinghka, Jacob Andreas, Joshua B. Tenenbaum
- Year: 2023
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12672

**Overview**
- Proposes mapping NL to structured LoT representations to enable compositional reasoning.

**Key Ideas**
- Intermediate schemas and probabilistic programs bridge surface text and cognition.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Prototype tasks demonstrating LoT translation and reasoning.

**Limitations**
- Coverage and scalability of schemas; integration with general LLMs.

**Discussion Questions**
- How to learn intermediate representations without heavy supervision?
- Faithfulness of translated programs to intended semantics.
- Combining LoT with retrieval and memory for richer models.
