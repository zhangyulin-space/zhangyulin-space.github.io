**Paper**
- Title: Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models
- Authors: Guanzhi Wang, et al.
- Year: 2023
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291

**Overview**
- Presents an LLM agent in Minecraft with curriculum generation and skill libraries.

**Key Ideas**
- Continual learning; tool-use; embodied skill acquisition.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Long-horizon tasks and performance vs. baselines.

**Limitations**
- Transfer beyond Minecraft; reliance on shaping and heuristics.

**Discussion Questions**
- How to generalize curriculum strategies to other environments?
- Balancing autonomy and safety in open-ended agents.
- Representing skills for reuse and composition.
