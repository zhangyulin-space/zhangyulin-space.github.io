**Paper**
- Title: Socratic Models: Composing Zero-Shot Multimodal Reasoning with Language
- Authors: Andy Zeng, Maria Attarian, Brian Ichter, Krzysztof Choromanski, Adrian Wong, et al.
- Year: 2022
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00598

**Overview**
- Composes pretrained models via multimodal-informed prompting to exchange information.

**Key Ideas**
- Modular zero-shot composition; multimodal prompting; external modules.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Egocentric video tasks; retrieval; captioning.

**Limitations**
- Calibration and reliability of cross-model exchanges.

**Discussion Questions**
- What interfaces best support multimodal prompting?
- How to ensure grounded reasoning across modalities?
- Role of active interaction for alignment.
