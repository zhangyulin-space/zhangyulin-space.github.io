**Paper**
- Title: Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM)
- Authors: Percy Liang, Rishi Bommasani, Tony Zhou Shijie, Kelly Zhang, et al.
- Year: 2022
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110

**Overview**
- Proposes multi-dimensional evaluation framework (accuracy, robustness, fairness, etc.).

**Key Ideas**
- Transparent reporting; comprehensive benchmarking across scenarios.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Benchmark suite and metrics; comparison across models.

**Limitations**
- Coverage breadth vs. depth; evolving task distributions.

**Discussion Questions**
- How to design psychometrically rigorous LLM evaluations?
- Role of adversarial/counterfactual tests to measure genuine reasoning.
- Calibration and validity checks in score reporting.
