**Paper**
- Title: Cognitive Biases in Large Language Models
- Authors: Katherine Collins, Albert Q. Jiang, Simon Frieder, Lionel Wong, Miri Zilka, Umang Bhatt, Thomas Lukasiewicz, Mateja Jamnik, Yuhuai Wu, Joshua B. Tenenbaum, et al.
- Year: 2024
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00118

**Overview**
- Surveys anchoring, framing, halo effects in LLMs; mitigation strategies.

**Key Ideas**
- Debiasing via training adjustments and inference controls.

**Methods / Evidence**
- Aggregated evaluations across tasks and model families.

**Limitations**
- Benchmark heterogeneity; transferability of mitigations.

**Discussion Questions**
- How to isolate cognitive-like biases from dataset artifacts?
- What deployment-time controls are practical and effective?
- Trade-offs between bias reduction and utility.
