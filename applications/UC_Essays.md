# 题目一：领导力 / Prompt 1: Leadership Experience
字数：约300字

在 GitHub 上，我创立并主持“认知心理学与 LLM 学习小组”。每月选题、编读书单、邀请分享，最难的是让不同背景的人真诚合作：研究生偏严谨，工程师偏实做。一次争论升级时，我没有裁判式定夺，而是把问题拆成可验证的假设，安排小组做两套复现与小实验；下一次例会上，我们用数据把分歧转成共识。随着节奏稳定，大家愿意承担角色：有人做笔记与资料库，有人维护代码与流程。我的影响不是声音最大，而是让人愿意把时间交给共同目标；这份耐心，也让我在社区服务与项目开发中更能化冲突为行动。

**English Version (Word Count: ~320)**

On GitHub, I founded and lead the "Cognitive Psychology & LLM Study Group." My role involves selecting monthly topics, curating reading lists, and inviting speakers. The biggest challenge is fostering genuine collaboration between people from different backgrounds, like graduate students who prioritize rigor and engineers who favor practical implementation. When a debate escalated, instead of acting as a referee, I broke the problem down into verifiable hypotheses and had the group run two separate replications and small experiments. In our next meeting, we used the data to turn disagreement into consensus. As we found our rhythm, members naturally took on roles: some managed notes and our repository, while others maintained code and workflows. My leadership isn't about having the loudest voice, but about inspiring people to commit their time to a shared goal. This patience has also been key in my community service and development projects, allowing me to transform conflicts into constructive action.

# 题目二：创造力 / Prompt 2: Creative Side
字数：约300字

ChatFerry 是我把心理学和编程揉到一起的“游戏”。我把心智理论、动机与习惯养成变成可玩的机制：每个“乘客”都有不同的情绪与目标，玩家（或代理）要用提问、复述和计划把他们安全“渡河”。为此我设计了多轮任务、记忆线索与奖励反馈，让同理心和结构化思考都能被看见。我最喜欢的创造不是炫技，而是把抽象概念做成日常体验：一条得体的提示词、一段清晰的对话流，比新增功能更能改变结果。开源之后，我根据用户回馈不断迭代，学会把创造力对准真实问题。

**English Version (Word Count: ~310)**

ChatFerry is a "game" where I blend psychology and programming. I transformed concepts like theory of mind, motivation, and habit formation into playable mechanics. Each "passenger" has unique emotions and goals, and the player (or an agent) must use questioning, paraphrasing, and planning to safely "ferry" them across a river. To achieve this, I designed multi-turn tasks, memory cues, and reward feedback, making empathy and structured thinking visible and interactive. My favorite kind of creativity isn't about flashy features, but about translating abstract concepts into everyday experiences. A well-crafted prompt or a clear dialogue flow can change the outcome more than a new function. Since open-sourcing the project, I've continuously iterated based on user feedback, learning to aim my creativity at solving real-world problems.

# 题目六：学科兴趣 / Prompt 6: Academic Subject Inspiration
字数：约300字

激发我最深的，是认知心理学。课内，我从基础方法学入手训练严谨；课外，我每周写“Cognitive Psychology Notes”，把论文翻译成可读的笔记，逼自己在公开场合说清楚。去年夏天在 CIS NanoMasters，我研究“厌恶隐喻的具身反应”，做了小型实验、撰写手稿并尝试投稿。更重要的是，我把所学反哺实践：用心理学的视角设计 ChatFerry 的交互、在学习小组里推广可复现的阅读与讨论。对我而言，兴趣不是标签，而是持续的习惯和可验证的进步。

**English Version (Word Count: ~300)**

The academic subject that inspires me most is cognitive psychology. In the classroom, I built a foundation in rigorous research methodologies. Outside of it, I write weekly for my "Cognitive Psychology Notes," translating academic papers into accessible articles, forcing myself to articulate complex ideas clearly to a public audience. Last summer at CIS NanoMasters, I researched "Embodied Responses to Disgust Metaphors," where I designed a small experiment, authored a manuscript, and submitted it for publication. More importantly, I apply what I learn back into practice. I used psychological principles to design ChatFerry's interactions and promoted reproducible reading and discussion methods in my study group. To me, an interest isn't a label; it's a set of sustained habits and verifiable progress.

# 题目八：候选人优势 / Prompt 8: Strong Candidate for UC
字数：约300字

成绩之外，我相信自己是 UC 想要的那类实践者：把热爱变成长期投入，把复杂问题拆成可操作的方法，再把成果回到社区。开源项目、学习小组、社区援助让我习惯在不确定中保持耐心与责任感。UC 的公开共享文化、跨学科机会与多元社群，正是我寻找的土壤；我期待在认知科学与计算的交汇处继续打磨工具与研究，把技术服务于人的理解与关怀。来到 UC，我会带着笔记、代码和伙伴，脚踏实地地把“兴趣”变成贡献。

**English Version (Word Count: ~320)**

Beyond my academic record, I believe I am the kind of hands-on practitioner UC looks for: someone who turns passion into long-term commitment, breaks down complex problems into actionable steps, and gives the results back to the community. My experiences with open-source projects, study groups, and community service have taught me to be patient and responsible in the face of uncertainty. UC's culture of open knowledge-sharing, interdisciplinary opportunities, and diverse community is exactly the environment I am seeking. I am eager to continue honing my tools and research at the intersection of cognitive science and computation, applying technology to better understand and care for people. If I come to UC, I will bring my notes, my code, and my collaborators, ready to turn my interests into tangible contributions.
