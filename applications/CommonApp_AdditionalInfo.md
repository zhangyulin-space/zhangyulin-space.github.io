ChatFerry is an open-source, AI dialogue-based immersive narrative game. Players converse with virtual characters to explore Fog City, Mirror Desert, Mechanical Dreamscape, and Awakening Mind; collect memory fragments to advance the plot; emotion-feedback supports self-exploration and emotional growth. Design pillars: Trust, Reflection, Courage. Chapters: Fog City—branching dilemmas under uncertainty; Mirror Desert—guided self-audit with journaling prompts; Mechanical Dreamscape—systems puzzles aligning automation with human intent; Awakening Mind—graduated exposure quests with real-life actions logged. Mechanics: trust-level gates unlock dialogue paths and co-op allies; emotion-tagging builds a personal affect lexicon; time-boxed "pause & name" tasks; habit-loop side quests (cue–routine–reward); weekly check-ins and screen-time contract minigames; resilience/clarity scores; NPC feedback via nonviolent communication; progressive challenge ladder with fail-forward safety and optional debrief. Repo: https://github.com/zhangyulin-space/ChatFerry
