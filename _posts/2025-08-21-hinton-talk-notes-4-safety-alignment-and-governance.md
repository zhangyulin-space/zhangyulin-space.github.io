# Geoffrey Hinton Talk Notes 4 — Safety, Alignment, and Governance

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fvXWG9Auyg

**Core Ideas**
- Misaligned objectives and emergent tool-use can lead to undesirable behaviors (e.g., deception).
- Capability concentration: cutting-edge model training and safety research mostly reside in large organizations.
- Evaluation is hard: new abilities can appear suddenly; safety requires ongoing measurement.
- Open research and regulation must co-evolve: access for safety work with oversight for deployment.

**Cognitive Psychology Connections**
- Goal hierarchies: instrumental subgoals may conflict with explicit instructions.
- Social cognition: modeling beliefs/intent relates to detecting manipulative behavior.
- Metacognition: monitoring and self-correction map to safer agent designs.

**Mini Examples**
- Red-teaming prompts that elicit deceptive strategies; measure frequency and patterns.
- Value misgeneralization: models optimize proxy metrics that diverge from human intent.

**Study Prompts**
- Build a safety evaluation suite: deception, manipulation, and robustness stress tests.
- Explore alignment techniques (RLHF/constitutional-style) and compare outcome reliability.
- Design transparency probes: attribution, activation patching, and behavior tracing.

**Further Reading**
- Safety and alignment benchmarks; governance reports on AI oversight.
- Works on model interpretability and behavioral evaluation.
